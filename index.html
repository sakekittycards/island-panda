Our Story

A Taste of the Islands on Sanibel

Island Panda was created to bring a fresh, fun, island-inspired dining experience to Sanibel. We serve colorful poke bowls, fresh sushi, and sizzling hibachi with a tropical twist — made for locals, visitors, beach days, lunch breaks, and easy family dinners.

Located on Periwinkle Way, our goal is simple: serve food that feels fresh, welcoming, and full of island flavor. Whether you're stopping in for a quick poke bowl, picking up sushi to-go, or grabbing hibachi after a day in the sun, Island Panda is here to make every meal feel a little more like vacation.

Fresh. Tropical. Made with aloha.
